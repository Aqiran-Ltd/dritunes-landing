Dr iTunes Consult (Global Limited)
Inspiring Digital Transformation Across Northern Ghana

Company Overview
Founded by visionary IT entrepreneur Alhassan Abdul-Hanan, popularly known as Dr. iTunes, Dr iTunes Consult (Global Limited) is a forward-thinking digital consultancy dedicated to empowering youth and businesses across Northern Ghana through education in digital innovation, cryptocurrency, and financial technologies.
Rooted in deep community values and futuristic thinking, the company is designed to bridge the gap between underprivileged regions and the rapidly evolving global digital economy.
 
Our Vision
To become the leading digital transformation consultancy in West Africa, pioneering inclusive tech education and innovation that uplifts communities and unlocks economic freedom.
 
Our Mission
•	To educate and mentor youth on cryptocurrency, blockchain, and digital finance.
•	To provide consultancy services in digital business setup, website development, and P2P trading.
•	To promote digital literacy and entrepreneurial skills in underserved regions.
•	To build digital ecosystems that create sustainable income streams for the African youth.
 
Core Services
1.	Cryptocurrency & P2P Trading Education
Expert-led workshops, seminars, and one-on-one mentorship on trading via platforms like Binance, Kucoin, and Bybit, and building financial independence through digital currencies.
2.	Tech & Digital Literacy Bootcamps
Practical ICT and coding sessions targeted at youth from rural and underserved communities in Northern Ghana.
3.	Business Digitalization Consulting
Tailored support for SMEs and startups looking to transition their operations online—ranging from e-commerce development, payment integration, to digital marketing strategies.
4.	Web & Software Development
High-quality, secure web applications, e-learning platforms, digital portfolios, and database systems—developed by our agile teams trained under the guidance of Dr. iTunes.
5.	Community Innovation Hubs
Plans underway to launch innovation hubs and ICT labs to provide access to tools, internet, and training for students and entrepreneurs.
 
Founder’s Background
Alhassan Abdul-Hanan (Dr. iTunes) is a Ghanaian tech entrepreneur, digital educator, and cryptocurrency trader. He served as President of the Computer Science Department and the Association of Information Technology Students (AITES) at Tamale Technical University, where he led several student-driven technology projects, including intelligent systems, web platforms, and election automation tools.
Dr. iTunes began his journey in cryptocurrency trading in 2018, and since then, has gained mastery in peer-to-peer (P2P) exchanges and blockchain technologies. His passion for education, impact, and digital inclusion led to the founding of Dr iTunes Consult to mentor the next generation of African tech innovators.
 
Key Achievements
•	Trained over 500 youth in crypto trading fundamentals and safe digital practices.
•	Spearheaded AITES Dream Tour, a tech-cultural project encouraging tech students to explore Ghana’s heritage.
•	Developed several in-house and client-based digital platforms and automated systems for education and business.
•	Built strategic partnerships with local institutions to promote tech awareness and financial inclusion.
 
Future Plans

•	Launch a scholarship program for underprivileged but talented youth to gain access to digital tools and education.
•	Partner with NGOs, government agencies, and global blockchain platforms to bring resources into rural Ghana.
